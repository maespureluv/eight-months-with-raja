The closed box makes a full turn to display its lettering. The ring rises from 7.9 to 9.5 seconds, floats for four seconds, and glides left from 13.5 to 15.7 seconds. It rotates beside the messages, then faces its heart toward the camera before the final zoom. The sequence ends at 23.4 seconds.
